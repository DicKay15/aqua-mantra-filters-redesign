# Discovery and current-site audit

## Company understanding

Aqua Mantra Filters is an Australian whole-house water-filtration supplier and installer serving Perth, Sydney and Adelaide. The business presents an end-to-end offer: system supply, delivery, licensed installation, pressure testing/calibration, ongoing maintenance and replacement reminders.

Current product families include the PureFlow 2 system, a three-stage whole-house system, three replacement-cartridge types and a replacement bundle. Public system specifications and installed-system prices are not currently available, so the redesign must not invent them.

The useful proof already present includes the Aqua Mantra logo, real product photography, 24 authentic installation images, five existing videos and Google review content. These assets should be presented with more context instead of being replaced by generic generated proof.

## Current information architecture

```text
Home
About Us
Products
Locations
  Perth
  Sydney
  Adelaide
Services
Gallery
Videos
Contact Us
  Review
```

The structure exposes pages but does not reflect the customer's decisions. Systems and replacement filters are mixed together, Reviews are hidden under Contact, post-purchase support has no first-class route, and the Locations parent is not a useful destination.

## What works today

- The end-to-end supply and installation offer is understandable.
- Current city coverage is visible.
- Authentic installation photography and video exist.
- Products and replacement-cartridge prices are available where known.
- The contact journey includes form, phone, WhatsApp and email.
- Canonical tags, basic schema and a skip-to-content link already exist.

## Highest-priority improvements

| Priority | Current issue | Improvement |
|---|---|---|
| High | No semantic H1 across audited pages | Give every route one intent-led H1 and logical H2/H3 structure |
| High | No unique meta descriptions | Create page-specific title, description and canonical metadata |
| High | Claims lack visible support | Pair claims with verified certification, licence, product test or authority source |
| High | Missing privacy and terms pages | Add reviewed legal content before public launch |
| Medium | Navigation follows pages, not decisions | Add Systems, How it works, Locations, Results and Guides groups |
| Medium | Products have little comparison or specification support | Separate installed systems and cartridges; add verified attributes and compatibility |
| Medium | Location pages are thin and partly generic | Add unique local context, service areas, proof, relevant systems, FAQs and authority sources |
| Medium | Contact form lacks privacy context and robust states | Add semantic grouping, validation, recovery, consent context and next-step expectations |
| Medium | Gallery filenames provide no meaning | Turn photos into installation snapshots with verified captions and descriptive alt text |
| Medium | Videos have no titles, context, captions or transcript paths | Curate by purpose and lazy-load with accessible controls |
| Medium | Legacy scripts and embeds create performance/privacy weight | Use a smaller modern frontend and load third parties only when needed |
| Low | Brand and product terminology is inconsistent | Establish controlled naming for WaterMark, whole-house and product families |
| Low | Copy contains errors and vague promotional language | Rewrite in precise, homeowner-friendly Australian English |

## Page-specific direction

- **Home:** national proposition, concern-led education, system comparison, interactive filtration story, process, city selector, installations, reviews, guides, FAQ and consultation CTA.
- **About:** real origin story, named team only when supplied, operating principles, credentials, service footprint and proof links.
- **Products:** installed systems separated from replacement filters, with verified specs, compatibility, maintenance and clear quote/purchase actions.
- **Perth, Sydney and Adelaide:** genuinely unique water context, suitable systems, service-area details, local proof, FAQs and consultation CTA.
- **Services:** assessment, recommendation, delivery, licensed installation, handover and aftercare, including verified inclusions and time expectations.
- **Gallery:** authentic installations in a responsive, accessible proof gallery. Do not fabricate project outcomes.
- **Videos:** named and described video items, poster frames, captions/transcript path and relevant product/service links.
- **Contact:** consultation-first form with clear next steps and phone/WhatsApp alternatives.
- **Reviews:** transparent Google source, authentic excerpts, proof themes and installation links.

## Benchmark synthesis

| Benchmark | Strong industry pattern to adapt | Do not copy |
|---|---|---|
| Water Analytics Australia | Evidence-led problem education and assessment framing | Dense menu, vague claims or unsupported reduction language |
| Complete Home Filtration | Assessment, installation and aftercare journey; visible service proof | Repetitive review feed or wellbeing causation claims |
| Puretec | Water-source and household-need decision logic; lifecycle support | Manufacturer-scale catalogue and dealer workflows |

## Human verification required before public launch

- WaterMark certificate number and covered products/components.
- Plumber licence holder and details for each state.
- Product specifications, testing standards and reduction claims.
- Warranty duration, exclusions and responsible party.
- Confirmed service coverage and suburb lists for all three cities.
- Whether commercial properties are supported.
- Installation pricing, finance options and response times.
- ABN, legal entity, approved address and professional email.
- Permission to reuse each review, gallery image and video.
- Final Privacy, Cookie, Terms, refund/cancellation and ecommerce policies.
