# Aqua Mantra Filters redesign: master brief

Last updated: 10 September 2026

## Outcome

Create a complete, professional and sophisticated redesign of Aqua Mantra Filters that preserves the existing brand and every required page, improves the customer journey and information architecture, supports local SEO, and feels alive through purposeful high-quality motion.

The finished experience must help an Australian homeowner understand their water concern, choose or ask for the right system, trust Aqua Mantra's installation capability, and book a free consultation.

## What the website should make people feel

**Calm, informed and confident.** A whole-house filtration system is a meaningful home-infrastructure purchase. Visitors need to feel that Aqua Mantra understands their concern, can explain the solution without pressure, and will install and support it correctly.

## Project scope, restated

- Understand Aqua Mantra's business, products, services, proof, customers and current website before designing.
- Audit and redesign every supplied page. No supplied page may be dropped.
- Document the current issues, proposed improvements, new information architecture, PRD, content plan, SEO plan, visual system, image plan, motion plan, implementation checklist and testing results.
- Preserve the Aqua Mantra name, theme cues and existing logo. Improve presentation without inventing a new brand.
- Study Water Analytics Australia, Complete Home Filtration and Puretec for proven industry patterns and useful elements.
- Reuse principles and functional patterns only. Do not copy their visual design, layouts or language.
- Do not invent business facts, performance claims, testimonials, certifications, awards, specifications, service coverage or pricing.
- Create an original Aqua Mantra experience from existing verified content, authentic client assets and clearly marked placeholders where business verification is still required.
- Build a custom interactive water hero with Three.js and a performant static/reduced-motion fallback.
- Use Framer Motion and GSAP for meaningful page, scroll and interaction choreography.
- Create a smooth animated filtration/process visualization using code-native SVG and motion.
- Plan all required generated images before generation, then create and integrate only the approved plan.
- Consider video only where it proves a real product, installation, maintenance step or customer outcome. Do not add decorative video.
- Create four useful industry/location-led articles with supporting imagery.
- Build responsive, accessible, SEO-ready pages with clear conversion paths.
- Create a repository under Dhrumil's GitHub account and keep project documentation with the implementation.
- Test every route, link, navigation state, form state, responsive layout, keyboard path, animation fallback, SEO field and performance-sensitive feature.
- Fix confirmed issues and repeat affected tests until the implementation passes or a genuine human-owned dependency remains.
- Keep a feedback log ready throughout the project and convert feedback into tracked actions.

## Reference-site rule

The references are an industry benchmark, not a design template.

We can reuse:

- Complete Home Filtration's clear assessment, installation and aftercare journey.
- Puretec's water-source and household-need decision logic.
- Water Analytics Australia's evidence-led education and problem taxonomy.

We must not reuse:

- Page layouts, visual compositions, copy, brand treatments or unique interactions.
- Unverified claims, awards, review counts, certifications or product specifications.
- Manufacturer/dealer complexity that does not match Aqua Mantra's installer-led business.

## Non-negotiable quality rules

- One clear H1 and unique metadata per route.
- Real proof only. No fake reviews, team members, case-study results or metrics.
- Water and health claims must be qualified and supported by authoritative evidence.
- The Three.js hero and all major motion must respect `prefers-reduced-motion`.
- Motion must explain flow, provide feedback or create hierarchy. It must never block reading or interaction.
- Main content remains usable if WebGL, JavaScript, video or third-party embeds fail.
- Minimum 44 px touch targets on mobile and visible keyboard focus on all controls.
- Generated lifestyle images may set context, but never represent a real Aqua Mantra customer, technician or installation.
- Authentic gallery photos must be used for installation proof.
- Forms require clear labels, validation, error and success states, and privacy context.
- Legal, licence, certification, warranty and claim details remain launch conditions until the client verifies them.
