# Content and SEO plan

## Positioning

Professionally supplied and installed whole-house water filtration, matched to the home, local water conditions and the customer's priorities.

Municipal water must not be framed as unsafe by default. Perth, Sydney and Adelaide utilities treat and monitor supplied water. Aqua Mantra's credible story is better taste and odour, filtered water from every tap, selected substance reduction only where exact product evidence supports it, scale management rather than automatic hardness removal, professional installation and ongoing support.

## Four initial guides

### 1. Perth hard water: filtration, scale management and softening

- Route: `/guides/perth-hard-water-filtration-vs-softening/`
- Target intent: Perth hard water filter, scale on shower screens, whole-house filtration Perth.
- Explain hardness, why it varies, what sediment/carbon filtration does, what scale-management media may do and what true softening does.
- Critical caveat: filtration must not be presented as removing hardness unless exact product evidence supports that mechanism. [Water Corporation guidance](https://www.watercorporation.com.au/Help-and-advice/Water-issues/Water-quality/Hard-water)

### 2. Sydney tap water, chlorine taste and whole-house filtration

- Route: `/guides/sydney-tap-water-chlorine-taste-filtration/`
- Target intent: chlorine water filter Sydney, Sydney tap-water taste, carbon filter chlorine.
- Explain treatment/monitoring, why taste/odour may be noticed, carbon filtration and the importance of exact cartridge test evidence.
- Do not describe Sydney broadly as a hard-water market. [Sydney Water analysis](https://www.sydneywater.com.au/water-the-environment/how-we-manage-sydneys-water/safe-drinking-water/water-analysis.html)

### 3. Adelaide home filtration: choose by source, suburb and goal

- Route: `/guides/adelaide-water-quality-home-filtration-guide/`
- Target intent: home water filtration Adelaide, whole-house water filter Adelaide.
- Explain source variation, postcode profiles, aesthetic considerations and how system selection begins with the actual property and concern.
- Avoid a single citywide contaminant claim. [SA Water drinking-water profile](https://www.sawater.com.au/water-and-the-environment/safe-and-clean-drinking-water/your-drinking-water-profile)

### 4. How often should whole-house filters be replaced?

- Route: `/guides/how-often-replace-whole-house-water-filters/`
- Target intent: how often replace whole-house water filter, replacement cartridges, servicing.
- Explain why intervals vary, capacity, usage, inlet quality, pressure-drop signs and safe replacement.
- Do not publish an Aqua Mantra interval until the applicable manual/product data is supplied.

## Structured data

- Home/About: `Organization`; Home also uses `WebSite`.
- Inner pages: `BreadcrumbList` matching visible navigation.
- Products: `Product`, with `Offer` only when current price and availability are visible.
- Services: `Service` with truthful `areaServed`.
- Videos: `VideoObject` only when required fields are visible and accurate.
- Guides: `Article`/`BlogPosting` with author and update date.
- FAQ markup is optional and must match visible content; do not rely on rich-result eligibility.
- Do not expect review stars from self-controlled LocalBusiness/Organization review markup. [Google review guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/structured-data/review-snippet)

## Local SEO

- Confirm whether Aqua Mantra has genuinely staffed operating bases in all three cities.
- Create Google Business Profiles only for eligible real locations/service-area operations. Do not invent offices or use virtual offices. [Google Business Profile guidelines](https://support.google.com/business/answer/3038177)
- Keep approved name, phone, address/service area, hours and URL consistent.
- Add genuine suburb/city installation captions and reviews as available.
- Build unique city copy from authority sources and client operations, not city-name substitution.
- Location-specific tracking must not break public NAP consistency.

## Compliance and evidence gate

- Every performance, health, material, origin, warranty, price and comparison claim needs evidence under Australian Consumer Law. [ACCC guidance](https://www.accc.gov.au/consumers/advertising-and-promotions/false-or-misleading-claims)
- Verify WaterMark and applicable lead-free status for the exact product/components. [WaterMark certification](https://watermark.abcb.gov.au/areas-interest/product-certification)
- Obtain exact test standard, lab, model, flow, concentration, capacity and interval before contaminant-reduction claims.
- Avoid medical promises and materially edited customer claims.
- Do not use generated imagery as staff, customer, facility, test or installation proof.
- Limit contact-form collection and use approved privacy wording. [OAIC Australian Privacy Principles](https://www.oaic.gov.au/privacy/australian-privacy-principles)
- Quote enquiries do not automatically create marketing consent. [ACMA spam guidance](https://www.acma.gov.au/avoid-sending-spam)
